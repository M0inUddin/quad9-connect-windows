# Quad9 artwork

`public/quad9-icon.svg` and `public/quad9-icon.png` are the unchanged `favicon.svg` and `favicon.png` files from the official Quad9 logo archive, downloaded 19 October 2026.

Source: [Quad9 media kit](https://quad9.net/news/media-kit/), [logo archive](https://quad9.net/news/media-kit/quad9_logos.zip).

`src-tauri/icons/icon.ico` and `src-tauri/icons/128x128.png` are size/format conversions of that PNG produced by the Tauri icon tool. The artwork and proportions are preserved.
